# Soft Signal — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, restrained single-page website for **Soft Signal**, a fictional independent journal about a more thoughtful relationship with technology. It should evoke a well-made print magazine, a quiet desk and a device that asks nothing of you.

## Art direction

Use pale lilac-grey paper (#e7e5e9), soft charcoal ink (#20211e), dusty violet (#b7a7cb) and acid lime (#d3ef70), with a near-black closing band. Set almost everything in DM Sans, with huge, medium-weight, tightly tracked headlines and single emphasis words in italic Playfair Display. Lay it out like an editorial spread: hairline ink rules between sections, small uppercase metadata, outlined pill links that fill lime on hover. The cover image is an inline SVG of a sculptural, speaker-like listening object in lime and violet, framed by magazine-style corner labels. A second art panel shows a violet sphere inside concentric circles on lime. No animation beyond smooth scrolling.

## Experience

Begin with a top bar (lowercase wordmark, three anchor links) and an issue line reading "Issue 01 / The attention issue". The split hero pairs "Less noise. More meaning." and an "Explore the issue" pill with the violet cover. Follow with a one-sentence statement of the journal's point of view set very large. Under "Inside the issue", present a featured essay beside the lime art panel with a link to the reading room, then three story columns (field note, object study, perspective), each with a category label, headline, summary and a native disclosure that expands a short paragraph. Close with a dark "Stay curious." contact band holding a "Write to us" email link to a placeholder address, and a footer labelling the site a fictional editorial concept. Build it without JavaScript.

Use semantic HTML, labelled illustrations, visible focus outlines, columns that stack below 800px, and a reduced-motion rule that turns off smooth scrolling.
