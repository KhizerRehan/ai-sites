# Moth Club — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, nocturnal and gently scholarly website for **Moth Club**, a fictional society and field guide for people who love the world after dark. It should evoke a porch light at dusk and a naturalist's specimen drawer.

## Art direction

Use a deep green-black night (#101914), warm cream (#e6e1cb), muted sage text (#a6b09a), antique gold (#d0b875) and dark olive hairlines (#48584a); switch to pale parchment for the field guide and a tan-gold band for the closing invitation. Set headlines in DM Serif Display with gold italic words, and use DM Sans for body text and small uppercase gold tags. The key image is an inline SVG moth: one detailed half-wing with veins, dotted texture and an eye spot, mirrored to make the pair, inside faint concentric halo rings.

## Experience

Open with a ruled navigation bar (lowercase wordmark split by a gold asterisk, four anchor links) and a two-column hero: tag, the headline "Come a little closer to the night.", a line of copy, a gold button and the moth with an italic caption. Add an introduction calling the club an imaginary gathering place. Build the parchment field guide as a bordered viewer: three toggle buttons (Emperor, Luna, Garden tiger) update the common name, Latin name, description and two facts (what to look for, habitat), and re-tint the same moth illustration with a colour filter. Continue with a three-step ritual for observing moths with care, a gold "See you after dark." band, and a footer noting it is a fictional club.

Make it responsive with breakpoints near 800px and 480px: stack columns, place the specimen art above its text and drop some nav links. Label the navigation, illustrations and controls, expose pressed states and show gold focus outlines on links and buttons.
