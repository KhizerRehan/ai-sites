# MONO MOUNTAIN — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, stark and technical website for **MONO MOUNTAIN**, a fictional alpine gear studio making "equipment for the long way out". It should evoke cold air, quiet ridgelines and the confidence of carrying only what matters.

## Art direction

Work almost in monochrome: near-black (#101311), pale grey-green paper (#eceee7), muted greys and a single acid lime accent (#daff00). Set uppercase headlines in Archivo Black with very tight tracking, body text in DM Sans, and labels, numbering and captions in uppercase Space Mono. Use two greyscale mountain photographs: one as the hero background fading into black from the left, one filling half of a split section. Draw the three products as flat dark inline SVG illustrations (a shell jacket, a backpack, a trail shoe), lifting slightly on hover.

## Experience

Begin with a black header (logo with a lime slash, anchor links, a mono tag reading "Est. imaginary") and a tall hero: lime eyebrow, the headline "Go beyond the known." ending in a lime full stop, a short paragraph, a lime button, and a ruled foot line with mono captions, one naming it a fictional outdoor equipment studio. Present the equipment as three numbered cards (Contour Shell, Traverse Pack, Ridge Low) with a category tag. Add a dark "Do more with less" statement beside the second photograph. Build a gear finder: three toggle buttons (a trail day, changing weather, going further) update a dark result panel with a product name, a sentence and a category line through a live region. Close with a lime manifesto, "Take the long way.", and a black footer with a fictional-concept label.

Make it responsive with breakpoints near 800px and 480px: stack grids and drop secondary captions. Label the illustrations, photo and button group, and expose pressed states.
