# Deep Blue — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, immersive dark website for **Deep Blue**, a fictional illustrated exhibit about the layers of the ocean. It should evoke a slow descent: fading sunlight, rising pressure and the hush of bioluminescence.

## Art direction

Use abyssal blue-black (#031723, #03111c), deep teal gradients, glowing aqua (#85f9e7) and pale sea-foam text (#e3fcfa), with one pale mint band (#c4f3e9) for a quote. Set headlines in light-weight Manrope with tight tracking and italic Georgia for emphasised words; use DM Sans for copy and tiny uppercase labels. The hero centres on an inline SVG jellyfish with a translucent gradient bell, trailing tentacles and an aqua glow, drifting slowly over faint diagonal light shafts. Use hairline aqua rules, pill buttons and a glowing orb holding a simple line diagram. Keep motion to the jellyfish float and small hover lifts.

## Experience

Lay a transparent header over a full-height hero: eyebrow, large headline, short invitation, a "Begin the descent" button and a "Scroll to discover" note. Follow with a story section explaining that most of the planet is out of sight. Then build the water-column explorer: a four-tab strip (Sunlight, Twilight, Midnight, Abyss, each with its depth range) that updates one panel with the zone's scientific name, a title, a short description, its light level and a creature to look for, beside the orb graphic. Add the mint quote band, a closing section with a button back to the layers, and a footer describing it as an imaginary exhibit inspired by the real ocean. There are no forms.

Under 750px show only the pill nav link, move the jellyfish above the copy and stack the tabs two-by-two. Implement tab semantics with roving tabindex and Arrow, Home and End keys, visible focus outlines, and stop the float and smooth scrolling under reduced-motion.
