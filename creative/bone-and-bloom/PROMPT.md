# Bone & Bloom — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, quietly gothic editorial website for **Bone & Bloom**, a fictional private tattoo studio "for the beautifully strange". It should evoke a naturalist's specimen book: botanical, intimate, hand-drawn and unhurried.

## Art direction

Keep the palette almost monochrome: soft black ink (#171814), bone paper (#eeeae0), stone and putty greys (#d3d0c4, #bbbbae) and olive-grey lines. Set the wordmark in the blackletter UnifrakturMaguntia, headlines in large, light Cormorant Garamond with italic emphasis, and labels in small, widely tracked uppercase DM Sans. Use hairline borders, square corners and generous margins. All imagery is fine-line SVG botanical illustration: a leafy stem and bloom inside a dotted circle with small star marks, plus a rotated circular seal. No photography or animation.

## Experience

Start with a slim dark announcement bar calling it a fictional studio concept, then a ruled header with wordmark, anchor links and a boxed "Make an inquiry" link. The hero has three columns: side notes left and right, the headline and botanical drawing in the centre. Follow with a ruled strip of three studio descriptors, an introduction, and an artists section built as a tab list of three names; choosing one updates the name, specialty, short bio and style tags beside a specimen illustration. Then a dark "process" section with three numbered steps, and a centred contact section whose button opens a modal dialog. The dialog holds a short form (name and idea) and a close button; on submit it shows a status message that the idea was only previewed locally and nothing was sent. Say it is a demo inside the dialog and beneath the button.

Under 800px show a hamburger that toggles the menu with aria-expanded and collapse everything to one column. Use tab roles with aria-selected, labelled form fields and a native dialog.
