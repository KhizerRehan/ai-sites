# Blue Hour — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, poster-like single-page website for **Blue Hour**, a fictional independent contemporary dance company. It should evoke the charged quiet before a performance: bodies in motion, feeling over words, and the beauty of the unfinished.

## Art direction

Use electric cobalt blue (#283bff), near-black ink (#090a10), warm off-white (#f5f4ee) and a single acid lime accent (#d5ff51). Set headlines in Space Grotesk, uppercase, enormous and very tightly tracked, with body copy in DM Sans and occasional Georgia italics for emphasis. The hero is a full-height greyscale, high-contrast dance photograph screened over blue, with the title stacked across it and its second word drawn as an outline only. Alternate full-bleed colour blocks, thin white rules and pill-shaped outline buttons. Motion is limited to a scrolling lime marquee and soft hover states.

## Experience

Open with a transparent header over the hero, an eyebrow naming it an imagined company, the giant title, a one-line statement and a pill link to the season. Follow with the lime ticker of slogans, then a blue "about" block with a large statement and a short company paragraph. List an imagined season of three pieces as ruled rows: dates, title, one-line description, venue and format tag, and a "Details" link that jumps to the closing section. Add a blue manifesto panel with two large overlapping circle outlines and a single pull quote, an off-white closing section inviting people to stay close, and a blue footer with the stacked logo and anchor links. State in the closing section and footer that the company and performances are fictional. There are no forms or bookings.

Below 700px collapse the navigation into a hamburger button that toggles the menu, updates aria-expanded and closes on link click; stack the grids and stop the marquee. Disable animation, transitions and smooth scrolling under reduced-motion.
