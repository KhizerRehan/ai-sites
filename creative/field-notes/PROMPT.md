# Field Notes — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, calm editorial website for **Field Notes**, a fictional illustrated woodland journal about looking closer at the forest floor. It should evoke damp moss, a slow ramble and a naturalist's pocket notebook.

## Art direction

Use deep forest green (#19382b), pale lichen lime (#d7ea70), soft paper (#f0efdc) and rust orange (#db633d). Set headlines in DM Serif Display with lime or olive italic emphasis, copy in DM Sans, and labels and metadata in uppercase Space Mono. Illustrate with flat inline SVG in a field-guide style: three capped mushrooms inside a dotted survey circle with crosshairs and leaf sprigs, plus small drawings of a mushroom, a fern and a mossy mound. Frame specimen drawings like plates, with thin borders and plus-sign registration marks. The page is entirely static: no animation and no scripted interaction.

## Experience

Begin with a dark green header and matching hero: mono eyebrow, large serif headline, a short paragraph and a lime "Explore the field guide" button beside the mushroom illustration. Follow with a lime strip of four mottos, then an introduction about the magic of the almost missed. Present the field guide as three specimen cards — the fly agaric, the unfurling fern, a little world of moss — each with a framed drawing, name, italic subtitle, one line of description and two mono tags such as "Observe only". Add a full-width orange ethos section urging visitors to observe without disturbing, with a clear warning never to eat a wild mushroom based on a picture or website. Close with an invitation, a button back to the top and a dark footer. No forms, filters or dialogs.

Under 760px hide all nav links except the pill and stack everything to one column. Give the hero illustration a text alternative.
