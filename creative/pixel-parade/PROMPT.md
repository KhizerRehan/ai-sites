# Pixel Parade — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, high-energy single-page website for **Pixel Parade**, a fictional two-day festival for independent video games. It should evoke a neon-lit arcade, sticker-bombed cabinets and the pull of "one more go".

## Art direction

Use a deep purple-black base (#17132d) with cream (#fcf5dd), electric lime (#d5ff46), hot pink (#ff5fbc), sky blue (#6fc7ff) and orange (#ff8b4c). Set headlines in Space Grotesk, uppercase and tightly tracked, and labels in Space Mono. Lay a faint grid over the dark hero and build a tilted CSS arcade cabinet (screen, joystick, buttons, coin slot) on a pink disc with a dashed orbit and a "100% good games" sticker. Use emoji for the screen sprite and oversized card artwork. Lime block buttons have a pink hard shadow that collapses on hover; card artwork tilts. A pink marquee scrolls.

## Experience

Start with a lime announcement bar naming the fictional festival and its dates, then a nav with a stacked wordmark, three links and a "Plan your visit" button. The hero reads "Press play on new." with links to the games and the mini-game. After the marquee, add a cream intro with three stats, then "The play list": three coloured cards for imaginary games, each with genre, title and a one-line pitch. Build a playable mini-game, Star Catcher: Start runs a 15-second countdown while a star button jumps to a random spot on the board each time it is caught; show time, score and a best score kept for the current tab, then offer "Play again". Close with a blue details section (when, where, what to bring) that says there are no real tickets or events, and a footer.

Use semantic HTML, visible focus outlines, a labelled star target, single-column layouts below 800px, tighter at 430px, and a reduced-motion rule that disables animations and transitions.
