# Sound of Rain — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, contemplative single-page website for **Sound of Rain**, a fictional small poetry ritual that gathers three-line poems out of the weather. It should evoke rain on a window, a misty lake at dusk and permission to slow down.

## Art direction

Use deep teal-green ink (#1e3438) and a near-black green (#101f24) against pale paper (#e6e9df), mist (#bacbc6), a sage hairline (#859d96) and a soft peach accent (#e6b69b). Set headlines and the poem in Playfair Display with italic emphasis; use DM Sans for body copy and small, widely tracked uppercase labels. The hero is a full-viewport inline SVG illustration of a lake with layered hills, pine silhouettes and a blurred moon glow, overlaid with a looping diagonal rain-streak animation and a film-grain texture. Keep it quiet: hairline rules, generous space, no photography.

## Experience

Overlay a lowercase wordmark and two links (The idea, Make a poem) on the hero, with the headline "Let it rain words." and a vertical scroll cue. Follow with the poem studio: an intro with three numbered steps beside a paper card showing the current poem. Give the card three mood toggles (Soft rain, After the storm, Rain at night), and "Make another poem" and "Copy poem" buttons. Each mood holds several options for each of three lines; build a poem by picking one at random per line, and regenerate immediately when the mood changes. Copy to the clipboard and confirm in a status line, with a fallback message if copying fails. Close with a sage interlude pairing an abstract ripple panel with a quote, a dark invitation back to the generator, and a footer.

Make it responsive, one column on small screens. Use pressed states on the mood buttons, a live region for the poem, visible focus outlines, and stop the rain under reduced motion.
