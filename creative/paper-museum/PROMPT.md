# Paper Museum — Creative Brief

Create an original, fictional online exhibition celebrating the endless possibilities of a single sheet of paper. The experience should feel like entering a contemporary design museum built from tactile, hand-cut paper.

## Art direction

Use an editorial exhibition identity with oversized refined serif typography, sharp modern labels, warm white gallery space, cobalt blue, poppy red, lemon yellow, and deep ink. Pair an asymmetric grid with oversized paper sculptures, real paper fibers, strong directional light, and dimensional shadows. Avoid a conventional cards-first SaaS layout. Build a visual narrative that moves from a single dramatic sculpture to an intimate collection, then to an explanation of the material and a closing invitation.

## Content and interaction

Lead with “A world made by hand” and an original large-scale paper sculpture. Introduce the museum as a fictional digital exhibition. Build a filterable collection exploring four works across Fold, Cut, and Layer techniques. Clicking each artwork opens an accessible detail dialog with title, technique, and a short fictional curatorial note. Add a section about the material and a simple visitor guide explaining how to use the collection. Use generous whitespace, precise rules, and unexpected overlaps. Do not claim real artists, exhibitions, or events.

## Quality

Build a complete responsive site for desktop, tablet, and mobile. Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable filters and dialog, visible focus states, meaningful alt text, and reduced-motion support. Optimize artwork and test filters and dialog behavior. Mark exhibition content as conceptual. Publish privately via Sites.
