# Ghost Frequency — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, eerie late-night website for **Ghost Frequency**, a fictional audio mystery podcast about radio transmissions that should not exist. It should evoke static, dead air and listening alone after midnight.

## Art direction

Use near-black (#080909), bone white (#edede7), ash greys and one acid yellow-green signal colour (#dbff00). Set headlines in Space Grotesk — the hero title uppercase, enormous and crushed tight, its second word a thin outline — and all labels, nav and buttons in uppercase DM Mono. Draw the imagery as hairline SVG: concentric radar rings with crosshairs and a jagged waveform spiking through the centre, over a dark radial glow and faint CRT scanlines. Use square corners, thin grey rules and flat acid blocks. Motion is limited to a scrolling ticker.

## Experience

Open with a ruled header and a near-full-height hero: a glowing dot labelled as a fictional audio mystery, the giant title over the signal graphic, a one-line teaser and a scroll cue. Run an acid marquee of slogans, then introduce the premise of a midnight radio host and unexplained transmissions. Build the episode archive in two columns: four numbered episode buttons with durations, and a pale "case file" card; selecting an episode updates the case number, title and synopsis. Follow with a pale "Behind the signal" section ending in mono fine print that it is a concept podcast and all stories and characters are fictional, a centred call to action linking back to the episodes, and a minimal footer. There is no real audio player or form.

Under 700px hide the plain nav links and stack the grids. Track the selected episode with aria-pressed, update the case file in a polite live region, show acid focus-visible outlines, and neutralise animation and smooth scrolling under reduced-motion.
