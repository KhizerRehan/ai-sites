# Odd Object — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, witty and gallery-clean website for **Odd Object**, a fictional "museum of everyday curiosities" devoted to beautifully unnecessary inventions. It should evoke a small design museum with a sense of humour and an invitation to look twice.

## Art direction

Use near-black ink (#191c18), warm off-white paper (#f4f2e8), sharp lime (#d9f754), soft pink (#efb4c3) and powder blue (#b8c9ec), plus a pale yellow tile and a lilac section. Set headlines in Space Grotesk medium at very large sizes with strong negative tracking, and body text in DM Sans; use hairline rules, an outlined pill button and underlined text links. Imagery is flat inline SVG with thick ink outlines: a smiling two-handled cup in the hero, then a winding spoon, an umbrella and a clock on legs, each on a pastel tile. Place the hero cup in a large lime egg shape with a round rotated sticker. Objects tilt slightly on hover.

## Experience

Start with a ruled header (lowercase wordmark, anchor links, a pill button) and a two-column hero: eyebrow, the headline "Look again." with a lime full stop, a short introduction and the cup. Present the collection as three staggered cards (The Detour Spoon, Portable Shade, The Late Clock), each with an object number, a one-line caption and a native disclosure labelled "Read its story". Follow with a lilac section describing the imagined museum, then a visit section with a bordered four-cell card: playful opening hours, free admission, a whimsical location and a note that this is a fictional museum concept. The page needs no scripting.

Make it responsive with breakpoints near 800px and 420px: stack grids and hide nav links except the pill. Label the navigation and every illustration, and show clear focus outlines on links, buttons and summaries.
