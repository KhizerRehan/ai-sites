# Checkmate Club — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, bold and friendly website for **Checkmate Club**, a fictional neighbourhood chess club for curious minds. It should evoke a welcoming game night: confident, a little cheeky, more about showing up than about ratings.

## Art direction

Use vivid violet (#5838f5), near-black ink (#17171c), warm paper white (#f8f7f2) and punchy lime (#dbff68). Set headlines in heavy, uppercase, tightly tracked Space Grotesk and body in DM Sans. The imagery is typographic: an enormous tilted chess knight glyph with a hard drop shadow on a violet panel, over a faint rotated checkerboard and concentric rings, with a round lime "All levels welcome" stamp. Use black pill buttons, thin black rules, outlined tags and hard offset shadows. There is no animation.

## Experience

Open with a header (knight logo, anchor links, "Come play" pill) and a split hero: headline, short welcome and two calls to action beside the knight artwork. Add a dark slogan band, then an introduction about chess being better shared. List three weekly club nights as ruled rows with day and time, name, description and audience tag, noting that the schedule is fictional. The centrepiece is a playable mate-in-one puzzle on violet: an 8x8 board of buttons with a black king, white queen and white king. The visitor selects the queen, then a destination; only the correct square moves her and announces checkmate, while others give a gentle retry message. Include a status line, a hint and a "Reset board" button. Close with a lime invitation stating the club is an imaginary concept, and a dark footer. No sign-up forms.

Stack the hero and puzzle under 800px. Give every square an accessible label with its coordinate and piece and a visible focus ring, and announce puzzle status through a polite live region.
