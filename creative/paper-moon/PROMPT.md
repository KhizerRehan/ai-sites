# Paper Moon — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, warm single-page website for **Paper Moon**, a fictional independent children's book publisher. It should evoke cut-paper picture books, bedtime reading and the curiosity of a child asking one more question.

## Art direction

Use warm paper white (#fffdf5) with navy ink (#182649), tomato red (#f35b3f), sunny yellow (#ffd64b), royal blue (#2859b7) and soft pink (#f5b7bf), in bold flat blocks. Set headlines in Fraunces, heavy and tightly tracked, and body copy in DM Sans with small uppercase eyebrows. Draw every illustration in CSS, like paper cut-outs: a yellow crescent moon, a cloud, an open book and asterisk stars in the hero; a cat, a sun and a whale on the book covers; a smiling yellow circle on a red panel. Use rounded pill buttons, 2px navy outlines and hard offset shadows. Keep motion to smooth scrolling and hover fills.

## Experience

Open with a yellow strip announcing an imaginary publishing house, then a header with a tilted two-line wordmark, three links and an "Explore the shelf" pill. The blue hero reads "Big worlds. Little hands." beside the moon-and-book scene, followed by a static red strip of mottos. Build the bookshelf with mood filter pills (All stories, For adventurers, For bedtime, For the curious) that show or hide three illustrated book cards, each with a title, one-line pitch, mood and age range; say the books are fictional. Continue with a pink "Small books. Big feelings." note from the studio, a yellow row of three principles and a navy closing invitation linking back to the shelf. The footer labels the site a fictional creative concept.

Use semantic HTML, pressed-state filter buttons in a labelled group, visible focus outlines, a grid that drops from three columns to two, then one, and a reduced-motion rule that turns off smooth scrolling.
