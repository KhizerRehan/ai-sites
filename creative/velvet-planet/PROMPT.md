# Velvet Planet — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, dreamy single-page website for **Velvet Planet**, a fictional retreat at the edge of another world. It should evoke a dusk-pink planet over quiet dunes and the pleasure of getting wonderfully lost.

## Art direction

Use deep indigo ink (#221d33) and near-black violet for the sky, warm cream (#f5eddf), rust (#b65c4d), dusty mauve and soft peach. Set headlines in Playfair Display with italic emphasis and tight tracking; use DM Sans for body copy and small, widely tracked uppercase labels. Build every image from CSS alone: a hero cosmos of layered radial gradients and a sparse star field, a huge softly lit planet with a thin tilted ring, two dark dunes in the foreground; a wide landscape band with a glowing sun on the horizon; and three abstract room tiles made of arches, domes and circles in the same dusk tones.

## Experience

Overlay a header on the hero with an uppercase wordmark and asterisk, two anchor links and an outlined "Plan a daydream". Centre the headline "A little further from everything." over the planet, with two small captions pinned to the bottom corners, one a scroll cue. Follow with an intro statement that names Velvet Planet as a fictional retreat, then the full-width landscape band with a caption. Next, a mauve "stay" section presenting three imagined rooms (The Moon Room, The Observatory, The Dune House), each with its tile, a one-line description and a mood tag. Close with a dark invitation that says plainly this is an imaginary place, with a button back to the top, then a footer repeating that it is fictional. No booking forms or prices; this page needs no JavaScript.

Make it responsive: on small screens enlarge the planet, hide the secondary links and stack the rooms. Give the landscape illustration a text alternative.
