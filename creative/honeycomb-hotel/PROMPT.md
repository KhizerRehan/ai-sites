# Honeycomb Hotel — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, sunny and graphic website for **Honeycomb Hotel**, a fictional urban bee club that wants cities to make room for pollinators. It should evoke a bold community poster: warm, playful and quietly encouraging.

## Art direction

Use honey yellow (#f6da37), near-black ink (#171813), cream paper (#f5f1df), warm orange (#ef7040) and sage green (#8b9d59) in flat colour blocks separated by 2px ink rules. Set huge uppercase headlines in Space Grotesk bold with very tight tracking and body copy in DM Sans; outline one word of the hero headline in ink with a cream fill. Use pill buttons and small uppercase eyebrows. All imagery is inline SVG: a chunky striped bee inside a large dark circle, and oversized flowers in front of a silhouetted skyline. Motion is minimal: one large flower glyph rotating very slowly.

## Experience

Start with a yellow navigation bar (stacked wordmark, anchor links, a dark pill button) and a two-column hero with the headline "Make room for bees.", a short pitch, a button and the bee with a round orange stamp. Add a static dark slogan band. Continue with an about section that admits the club is make-believe and lists three numbered habits; a garden section pairing the city-flowers illustration with copy; and an orange "tiny garden planner" where three toggle buttons (a sunny sill, a balcony, a garden patch) swap the title and advice in a shadowed cream card through a live region. Close with a dark invitation containing the spinning flower and a line stating the project is fictional, then a yellow footer.

Make it responsive: below 760px stack every section, hide nav links except the pill and hide the decorative flower. Expose pressed states, label the illustrations, and stop the rotation and smooth scrolling for reduced-motion users.
