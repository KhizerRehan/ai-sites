# Fable Farm — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, warm and whimsical website for **Fable Farm**, a fictional flower farm celebrating the seasons. It should evoke a little wildness and a lot of wonder: slow growing, joyful gathering and flowers for ordinary days.

## Art direction

Use olive-black ink (#292b20), buttery cream (#f9f6dc), lime (#d5e85a), rose pink (#f08ba7), bright orange (#ff713e) and pale duck-egg blue (#b5d9d5). Set headlines in Fraunces, large and tightly tracked with terracotta italic emphasis, and copy in DM Sans with small uppercase eyebrows. Illustrate everything as flat inline SVG with dark green outlines: daisy-like flowers built from rotated ellipse petals, leafy stems and a yellow sun, set inside an arched pink panel. Use outlined pill buttons, round rotated stamps and full-bleed colour blocks. There is no animation.

## Experience

Open with a ruled header and a split hero: headline, short paragraph and "Meet the flowers" button beside the arched garden illustration with a "Grown with wonder" stamp. Add a dark band of slogans and a large belief statement. Build the seasonal field guide on lime: four tabs (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter) that swap the featured flower's label, name, description, "Feeling" and "Palette" facts, and recolour both the illustrated petals and the panel behind them. Follow with a split story section — a flower in a pink circle on orange, copy on pale pink — then a centred pale-blue closing section that says Fable Farm is a fictional flower-farm concept, and a dark footer with an oversized wordmark. Nothing is for sale and there are no forms.

Under 800px collapse the nav into a round hamburger that toggles with aria-expanded and shuts when a link is chosen; stack every grid. Use tab roles with aria-selected and a polite live region on the flower panel.
