# HOT MESS — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, loud and cheeky brand website for **HOT MESS**, a fictional small-batch hot sauce company with the line "Good food. Bad decisions." It should evoke a sauce label blown up to poster size: hot, messy and confident.

## Art direction

Use chili red (#ee3021), acid yellow (#f3fa31), warm cream (#fff5df), dark brown-black ink (#251111) and soft orange (#ff9947), in flat colour blocks divided by 3px ink borders. Set uppercase headlines in Archivo Black with tight tracking and body copy in DM Sans at heavy weights. Build the hero bottle from CSS shapes (ridged cap, neck, orange jar, cream label), tilted on a yellow sun disc with a hard ink drop shadow. Draw the chili pepper as inline SVG. Motion: a scrolling ticker and small hover lifts.

## Experience

Open with a yellow header (stacked wordmark, anchor links, pill button) and a red two-column hero with the headline, a one-line pitch, a "Meet the sauces" button and the bottle. Follow with a dark marquee ticker and a short story section stating the company is fictional. Then build "The Lineup" on yellow: a tab list of three sauces (Sunny Side, Red Flag, Afterburn) controlling one panel that updates the flavour descriptors, name, description, a five-segment heat meter with a spoken label and a "good on" pairing line, while recolouring the pepper and its background. Tabs respond to clicks and to left/right arrow keys with roving focus. Continue with a dark manifesto and quote card, then a yellow "Stay saucy." footer repeating the fictional-brand note.

Make it responsive: below 750px stack all grids and hide nav links except the pill. Label the navigation, tab list and illustrations, announce panel changes through a live region, and remove animation, transitions and smooth scrolling for reduced-motion users.
