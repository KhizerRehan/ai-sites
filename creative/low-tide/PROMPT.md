# LOW TIDE — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, calm and editorial website for **LOW TIDE**, a fictional coastal ceramics studio making objects for everyday rituals. It should evoke sea air, wet clay and slow mornings.

## Art direction

Use deep teal (#143f42) as the only ink, pale seafoam (#b6dbd5), a light mint off-white (#eef5ec) and darker teal blocks (#183f40). Set headlines in DM Serif Display, mixing upright and italic words, at large sizes with tight leading; use DM Sans for the semibold wordmark, small uppercase tracked labels and body text. Keep the layout airy, with outlined pill buttons and thin rules. Use three photographs: hands shaping a vessel on a wheel, finished ceramic vessels, and studio tools. Crop the hero photo into a tall arch and overlay a small rotated round stamp reading "made slowly by hand". Collection images zoom slowly on hover.

## Experience

Begin with a dark announcement bar calling it an imaginary studio by the sea, then a seafoam masthead with wordmark and three anchor links. The hero pairs an eyebrow, the headline "Made for slower days.", a short line and a pill button with the arched photo. Follow with a static row of short phrases; a studio note with a large serif statement; a two-card collection where the second card sits lower; a split "approach" section with a full-height photo beside copy on dark teal; and a centred invitation whose button is a mail link to a placeholder address. End with a dark footer with navigation and a line labelling it a fictional studio concept.

Make it responsive: below 760px stack all sections and replace the nav with a "Menu" button that toggles a dropdown, updates its expanded state and label, and closes when a link is chosen. Give every photo descriptive alt text.
