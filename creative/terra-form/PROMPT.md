# TERRA FORM — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, confident single-page website for **TERRA FORM**, a fictional speculative landscape architecture studio exploring a more generous relationship with land. It should evoke mist over a valley, a site plan pinned to a studio wall and the patience of designing for decades.

## Art direction

Use forest ink (#19291f), bone paper (#e7e4d6), burnt orange (#eb5d2f) and a muted lime-sage (#c9d278). Set huge, tightly tracked uppercase headlines in Space Grotesk and body copy in DM Sans, with small uppercase eyebrows. Split the hero in two: on the left "LAND IS A VERB." with the last word in orange and a circular outlined link; on the right a misty mountain photograph crossed by a tilted white ellipse and tiny captions. Elsewhere rely on hard one-pixel rules, flat colour fields and an inline SVG concept plan of curving paths, contour lines, concentric rings and orange planting beds.

## Experience

Use a ruled header with the wordmark, two anchor links and an outlined "Start a conversation". After the hero add an orange three-phrase strip and a point-of-view statement. Build a dark "Groundwork." section around a project viewer: the SVG plan beside an info panel, with three toggle buttons (Common Ground, Second Nature, Rain Commons) that swap the project type, title, description, location and scale and recolour the plan's background for each project. Follow with "Less control. More possibility." and three numbered principles, then an orange closing section with a giant headline, a mail link to a placeholder address and footer lines calling it a concept project of imagined places. No contact form.

Make it responsive: below tablet width stack the hero and grids and replace the navigation with a "Menu" button whose label flips to "Close". Use pressed states on the project buttons, text alternatives for imagery, and disable transitions under reduced motion.
