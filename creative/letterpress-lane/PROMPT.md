# Letterpress Lane — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, tactile and warm website for **Letterpress Lane**, a fictional neighborhood print workshop devoted to ink on paper. It should evoke ink-stained hands, heavy stock and the charm of small imperfections.

## Art direction

Use deep green-black ink (#1d2724), cream paper (#efe8d6), vermilion red (#d44430), indigo blue (#293f70) and marigold yellow (#ecc04d), like a three-colour print run. Set uppercase headlines in Archivo Black with tight tracking, drop in red phrases in Playfair Display bold italic, and use DM Sans elsewhere. Lay a fixed, subtle paper-grain noise texture over the whole page. Use 2px ink borders, hard offset shadows and solid rectangular buttons. The only illustration is an inline SVG vintage printing press on a red disc, placed on a slightly rotated marigold card. Motion is almost absent.

## Experience

Start with a bordered header (logo with one word in red, three anchor links) and a two-column hero: the headline "Good things leave a mark.", a short pitch, a button and the press card. Add a static red slogan ribbon. Follow with an introduction calling the workshop imaginary; an indigo services list (paper goods, posters and prints, workshop days); and the "ink lab" mixer. Three toggle buttons with colour chips (Vermilion, Indigo, Marigold) can be combined freely: average the selected inks into a large swatch carrying "Make a mark.", show the recipe as names joined by plus signs in a live region, and fall back to paper colour and "No ink selected" when none are chosen. Close with a marigold invitation restating that the workshop is fictional, and a dark footer.

Make it responsive: below 800px stack every grid and let the nav wrap. Label the illustration and ink group, expose pressed states, and switch off smooth scrolling and the swatch transition for reduced-motion users.
