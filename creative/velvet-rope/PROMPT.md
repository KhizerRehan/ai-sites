# Velvet Rope — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, fashion-editorial single-page website for **Velvet Rope**, a fictional independent archive celebrating clothing's past lives. It should evoke a rail of well-loved garments and the romance of pieces that have already lived a little.

## Art direction

Use near-black ink (#171312), blush paper (#f5ede7), rose pink (#f2afbc) and lipstick red (#b72f27). Set large, tightly tracked headlines in Playfair Display with red or rose italic emphasis; use DM Sans for body copy and small bold uppercase labels. Compose it like a magazine spread: a split hero with a tall fashion portrait, a rotated rose circular seal overlapping the image edge, hard black rules, full-bleed colour bands and three photographs (a tailored portrait, a look in warm light, clothes on a rail).

## Experience

Open with a black announcement bar, then a ruled header with the wordmark, three anchor links and an "Explore the eras" link. The hero reads "Good taste never gets old." beside the portrait, followed by a red strip of repeated slogans. Tell the story in a two-column intro that names the archive as imaginary. Build the archive section around "Pick your era.": a full-width row of four decade tabs (the 60s, 70s, 80s and 90s) that swap the photograph, decade label, headline, paragraph and two detail fields (the mood, the signature), announced through a live region. Follow with a dark manifesto split beside the rail photograph ("Wear it. Love it. Pass it on."), and finish with a rose footer that doubles as the visit section: a giant "See you around." and a line stating it is a fictional fashion archive. No shop or contact form.

Make it responsive: on small screens hide the centre links and stack the hero, archive feature and manifesto. Give the tabs selected states and all photographs alt text.
