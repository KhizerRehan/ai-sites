# Lunar Laundromat — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, bubbly and retro-cosmic website for **Lunar Laundromat**, a fictional neighborhood wash club that makes laundry day feel like a small orbit. It should evoke bright sticker art, spinning drums and a cheerful place to sit while the cycle runs.

## Art direction

Use deep indigo ink (#25234e), hot orange (#ff672f), butter yellow (#ffe25e), candy pink (#ff9dba), cream (#fff4dc), sky blue (#8bcfdf) and soft purple (#7456a0), one colour per full-width section with 3px indigo borders. Set rounded headlines in Fredoka with tight tracking and body text in DM Sans. Give buttons and cards hard offset shadows. Build the hero washing machine in CSS: a tilted yellow body with a control panel and a blue porthole holding a slowly rotating conic-gradient bundle of clothes, circled by an orbit ring with star glyphs. Motion: the spinning drum and a marquee ticker.

## Experience

Open with a cream header (tilted logo, anchor links, a pill button) and an orange hero with the headline "Give it a little spin.", a short pitch, a button and the machine. Follow with a dark slogan ticker. Add a story section beside an arch-topped pink panel of socks. Then a blue "What's in the basket?" chooser: three radio-style toggle buttons (everyday pile, delicate things, big cozy stuff) update a yellow result panel with a named cycle, a description and a care-label reminder through a live region. Continue with three pink "wash day" steps and a purple visit section with a tilted cream card listing illustrative opening hours and a note that the laundromat is a fictional design concept.

Make it responsive with breakpoints near 850px and 480px: stack all grids and hide nav links except the button. Label the machine illustration, expose pressed states, and collapse animation for reduced-motion users.
