# Wild Current — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, outdoorsy single-page website for **Wild Current**, a fictional field guide to rivers, wild places and the care that keeps them wild. It should evoke cold moving water, forest banks at first light and a well-thumbed map.

## Art direction

Use deep river green (#112927), cream (#eeeede), pale lime green (#cedb91), a signal orange (#f26c42) and sage hairlines (#8b9a84). Set huge uppercase headlines in condensed Oswald, with occasional Georgia italics, and DM Sans for body copy and tracked uppercase eyebrows. Use a full-height river photograph under dark gradients for the hero and a forest photograph later; everything else is flat colour, thin rules and a map panel drawn in SVG with repeating contour lines.

## Experience

Put the navigation inside the hero: wordmark, two anchor links and an outlined "Explore the guide". The hero reads "Follow the water." with a lime button. Follow with an intro statement, then the route explorer on a pale green band: a dark panel listing three numbered route buttons (Fernwater Bend, Stone & Pine, North Fork) beside a map panel showing a winding river path with orange start and end points, a caption, and a card with the route name, three descriptors and a pace tag. Selecting a route redraws the path, moves the points and updates all text. Note that the routes are fictional, not navigational guidance. Then a stewardship split with the forest photograph and three numbered principles, an orange closing band, and a dark footer marked as a fictional concept. No forms.

Make it responsive: below tablet width show a "Menu" button that toggles the links and flips to "Close", and stack the explorer and split sections. Use pressed states on route buttons, a live region for the map, and text alternatives for imagery.
