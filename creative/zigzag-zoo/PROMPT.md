# Zigzag Zoo — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, joyful single-page website for **Zigzag Zoo**, a fictional illustration studio making wonderfully odd creatures. It should evoke a sticker sheet, a child's best drawing and the freedom of not colouring inside the lines.

## Art direction

Use indigo ink (#242054) with sunny yellow (#ffdc46), bubblegum pink (#ff73aa), baby blue (#b8e5fa), orange (#ff8746), lilac (#bca3ef) and off-white paper (#fffdf3). Set chunky, tightly tracked headlines in rounded Fredoka and body copy in DM Sans. Draw every creature as inline SVG with thick indigo outlines, simple dot eyes and smiles. Use two-pixel indigo borders between colour-blocked sections, pill buttons, sparkle and asterisk glyphs (one slowly spinning) and a tilted two-line wordmark. No photography.

## Experience

Use a bordered header with the wordmark, three anchor links and a "Say hello" pill. Split the hero: yellow copy panel ("A little wild. A lot wonderful.") beside a pink panel with a long-necked striped creature. Add an indigo slogan strip, a short intro, and a three-card gallery introducing Pickle, Wiggles and Bean, each with a persona label and one-line bio. Then the play studio on indigo: a rounded pink card with a live creature preview and controls to pick a shape (Round, Tall, Squish) and a colour (Lilac, Sky, Peach), plus a "Surprise me!" button that randomises both; update the body outline and fill instantly and announce the result in a sentence. Follow with a blue "what we do" section listing four services, and a pink closing section that states this is a fictional studio concept and links back to the builder, not a form.

Make it responsive: on small screens keep only the pill in the navigation and stack the hero, gallery and studio. Use fieldsets with legends, pressed states, a live status, text alternatives for each creature, and no animation under reduced motion.
