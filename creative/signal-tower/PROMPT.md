# Signal Tower — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, bold single-page website for **Signal Tower**, a fictional independent community radio station. It should evoke late-night dials, hand-picked records and the warmth of a human voice between songs.

## Art direction

Use a near-black page (#161615) with signal red (#ee382b), warm paper (#f1eddf) and sharp yellow (#e7e853). Set headlines in Space Grotesk, uppercase, enormous and tracked very tight; set labels, times and tags in DM Mono, uppercase and letterspaced. Give it the look of a printed broadcast schedule: hairline rules, square outlined buttons, a crosshair-in-a-circle logo mark and red dot eyebrows. Illustrate with one CSS-built vintage radio, tilted on a red gridded panel with a hard shadow: a nameplate, an "ON AIR" badge, an 88 to 108 FM tuning scale with a red needle, a perforated speaker and two knobs. A paper-coloured marquee scrolls continuously.

## Experience

Begin with a header holding the wordmark, two mono links and an outlined "Explore the shows" button. The split hero pairs "Turn it up. Tune in.", a red call to action and two small facts (broadcasting from "Everywhere & nowhere", made for "Curious ears") with the radio. After the marquee, build the schedule, "What's on the dial.": day tabs (Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Sunday) that render that day's three shows as rows with a red time, show name, one-line description and a boxed genre tag. Follow with a yellow manifesto section, "Good radio belongs to everyone.", led by a giant rotated asterisk and a link back to the shows. Finish with a footer and a final line stating that this is a fictional radio station concept with no live broadcast connected.

Use semantic HTML, tab and tabpanel roles with selected states and a live region for the listings, visible yellow focus outlines, and layouts that stack below 800px.
