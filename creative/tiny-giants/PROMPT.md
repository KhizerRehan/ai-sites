# Tiny Giants — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, charming single-page website for **Tiny Giants**, a fictional miniature model-making studio that builds small worlds with big ideas. It should evoke leaning in close to a tiny house on a workbench and the feeling that someone lives behind the little door.

## Art direction

Use warm ink (#25231f) on cream paper (#f6f1e4) with bright lime (#d2ef42), coral (#ff7959) and a soft sky blue (#95d6d3). Set enormous, tightly tracked headlines in bold Space Grotesk and body copy in DM Sans, with small uppercase eyebrows. Draw all imagery in code: the hero scene is a CSS illustration of a peach house with a red roof, two trees, a sun, a cloud and a green hill on a sky-blue panel, tagged with a small paper label. Add a rotated circular stamp, pill buttons and flat colour-blocked sections. No photography, no animation.

## Experience

Use a simple header with a stacked lowercase wordmark and three anchor links, the last styled as a dark pill. The hero reads "Small worlds. Big feelings." above the stamp and the scene. Follow with an intro that names the studio as imaginary. Then a dark collection section with a viewer: an illustration panel beside a text panel, and three round numbered tabs that switch between The Greenhouse, The Corner Shop and The Lookout, each swapping its own inline SVG illustration, background colour, category line, title and description. Next, a lime process section with three steps (Dream it, Build it, Bring it to life), and a coral closing band whose pill links back to the collection, not a form. Finish with a short footer.

Make it responsive: on small screens show only the pill in the navigation and stack every grid. Give the tabs selected states and names, and describe the hero scene for screen readers.
