# Daybreak Cycles — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, sunny poster-style website for **Daybreak Cycles**, a fictional neighbourhood bicycle workshop and riding club. It should evoke early starts, long detours and the easy optimism of taking the long way home.

## Art direction

Use sunrise yellow (#f8c52a), burnt orange (#e75c2e), deep green-black ink (#152a2a) and warm cream paper (#fff7dd). Set headlines in bold, uppercase, tightly tracked Space Grotesk at poster scale and copy in DM Sans. The single illustration is an inline SVG line drawing of a classic road bicycle with spoked wheels, sitting on a tilted orange ellipse with a hard drop shadow and a rotated dark round stamp. Use thick ink borders, outlined pill buttons that invert on hover, and sun and asterisk glyphs as icons. There is no animation beyond smooth scrolling.

## Experience

Begin with a yellow header flowing into a yellow hero: eyebrow, three-line headline with one orange word, a short description set to the side, and the bicycle artwork. Add a dark band of slogans. Introduce the workshop with a large statement and a link to the services. Present three services on dark — Keep it rolling, Make it yours, Ride together — as ruled columns with glyph icons. Then an orange "What's your kind of ride?" section with a chooser card: three toggle buttons (Take it easy, Find a new road, Get around town) that swap a recommended ride title and description. Close with a yellow "See you out there" section stating that Daybreak is a concept workshop, and a dark footer repeating that it is fictional. No bookings or forms.

Under 760px stack the grids and hide the nav pill. Give the bike a text alternative, track selection with aria-pressed and announce the result through a polite live region.
