# Golden Hour — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, warm editorial website for **Golden Hour**, a fictional independent film lab for photographs worth holding onto. It should evoke late sun, film grain and happy accidents.

## Art direction

Use dark roast brown (#201711), warm paper (#f3e7d5), burnt orange (#ec663b), deep rust red (#b94327) and soft amber tan (#e0a969). Set headlines in DM Serif Display, very large with peach or rust italic emphasis, and copy in DM Sans. Lead with photography: a full-bleed misty sunset landscape under a dark gradient, a hazy mountain landscape, and a photographer holding a camera, all slightly muted. Use thin tan rules, pill buttons and a filmstrip-style orange band with thick dark borders. Motion is limited to a soft filter transition on the film-mood photo.

## Experience

Start with a dark header and a near-full-height photo hero: an eyebrow marking it a concept, a large headline, a short paragraph and an orange pill. Add the filmstrip band of mottos. On paper, introduce the imagined neighbourhood lab, then lay out three numbered steps from choosing a roll to letting the lab work. Build the film counter on rust red: a photo beside a detail panel with three toggle buttons (Soft & sunlit, Bold & bright, Black & white) that change the title, description, "Feel" and "Best for" values and apply a matching sepia, saturated or greyscale CSS filter to the photo. Follow with a split story section beside the camera photo, repeating that it is a fictional concept, an amber closing band and a slim footer. No orders or forms.

Under 760px hide the plain nav links and stack every grid. Give photos alt text, track the chosen mood with aria-pressed, announce the description via a polite live region, show focus-visible outlines, and drop transitions and smooth scrolling under reduced-motion.
