# Still Water — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, calm single-page website for **Still Water**, a fictional neighbourhood bathhouse devoted to the art of slowing down. It should evoke warm water, quiet rooms, soft daylight and an afternoon with nothing scheduled.

## Art direction

Use deep forest greens (#182b28, #122622) with warm cream (#e9e9dc), pale sage (#dbe3d9) and translucent hairlines. Set headlines in light-weight Newsreader with italic emphasis and tight tracking; use DM Sans for body copy and small, widely tracked uppercase eyebrows, with a tiny Manrope sub-label under the lowercase wordmark. Lead with a full-height photograph of the bathhouse under a dark left-to-right gradient, and use a second interior photograph for the quiet room. Keep the layout spacious and editorial: asymmetric grids, thin rules, almost no motion.

## Experience

Place a transparent header over the hero with the wordmark, anchor links and an outlined "Visit information" link. The hero carries "Make room for stillness." and a scroll cue. Follow with an intro statement, then a dark "spaces" section showing two captioned photographs (The bathing room, The quiet room) in an offset grid. Next, a ritual guide: a heading beside a four-step accordion (Arrive, Wash, Soak, Rest) with numbered buttons, a plus/minus symbol, the first step open, and each step toggling independently. Then a pale visit section with a short definition list of practical details, a button back to the ritual, and a small line stating that Still Water is a fictional bathhouse concept. End with a dark footer. Do not include a booking form.

Make it responsive: below tablet width replace the navigation with a "Menu" button that opens a dropdown and closes when a link is chosen, and stack every grid. Use semantic sections, alt text on photographs, expanded states on the accordion and menu, and disable smooth scrolling under reduced motion.
