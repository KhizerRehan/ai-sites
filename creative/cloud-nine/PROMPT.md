# Cloud Nine — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, cheerful picture-book website for **Cloud Nine**, a fictional kite shop and flying club for people who like to look up. It should evoke a breezy afternoon, a bit of string and uncomplicated joy.

## Art direction

Use bright cobalt blue (#1059d1), soft sky blue (#a9def4), navy ink (#12315d), cream (#fff9e5), poppy red (#f65b50) and sunshine yellow (#ffd944). Set headlines in rounded Fredoka and copy in DM Sans. Everything is flat inline SVG illustration with navy outlines: diamond, delta and round kites with ribbon tails, cloud squiggles, and a hillside scene of two figures flying kites. Use yellow pill buttons with hard navy offset shadows that press in on hover, a zigzag edge under the hero, arched panels and a rotated round stamp. The hero kite floats gently; card kites tilt on hover.

## Experience

Open with a blue announcement bar, a sky-coloured header, then the hero: headline, short line, "Meet the kites" pill and the floating kite with a "Good things are up" stamp. Add a red slogan band and a short hello that says Cloud Nine is an imaginary shop and club. Show the collection on yellow as three illustrated cards: The Daydreamer, The Zoomer and The Sunseeker. Then the kite studio on blue: four round colour swatches (poppy red, sunshine yellow, meadow green, candy pink) that recolour a preview kite and update a "Your kite" label. Finish with a coral club section beside the hillside illustration, noting it is a fictional concept, and a navy footer. No shop or forms.

Under 800px collapse the nav behind a "Menu" button that toggles with aria-expanded, and stack every grid. Track the chosen colour with aria-pressed, announce it via a live region, and remove animation and transitions under reduced-motion.
