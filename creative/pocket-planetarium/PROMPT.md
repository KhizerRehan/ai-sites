# Pocket Planetarium — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, friendly single-page website for **Pocket Planetarium**, a fictional pocket-sized planetarium: a little interactive tour of the real solar system. It should evoke a children's science museum, a well-loved field guide and the wonder of looking up.

## Art direction

Use a cream page (#faf6ea) with deep indigo ink (#17163d), a violet night panel (#292657), soft pink (#f5a0bb), golden yellow (#f5d457) and periwinkle accents. Set tightly tracked headlines in Space Grotesk and body copy in DM Sans with small uppercase eyebrows. Draw everything in CSS: a night-sky panel with one large rounded corner, concentric orbit rings, a smiling sun and eight flat planets (striped Jupiter, ringed Saturn, a green continent on Earth). Use pill buttons and a rounded fact card. Planets scale up with a white outline on hover or selection.

## Experience

Begin with a header (asterisk wordmark, an about link, a "Start exploring" pill), then a split hero: "Big space. Small adventures." beside the solar system, where each labelled planet is a button. Add a static pink strip of mottos. Build the "cosmic field guide": eight planet pills beside a fact card showing an orb, a nickname, the name, a short accurate description, position from the Sun and length of year. Choosing a planet from either place updates the card, recolours the orb and card background and syncs both sets of controls; choosing from the hero also scrolls to the guide. Note that illustrations are imaginative, not to scale, and years approximate. End with a rounded yellow "Keep looking up." note with a dotted stamp, and an indigo footer.

Use semantic HTML, labelled planet buttons, pressed-state pills, a live fact card, visible focus outlines, layouts that stack at 900px and tighten at 560px, and respect reduced motion when auto-scrolling.
