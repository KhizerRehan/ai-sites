# ATLAS OBSERVATORY — creative brief

Create an original, immersive website for Atlas Observatory, a fictional place devoted to the wonder of looking up. The experience should feel like stepping into an antique astronomical instrument reimagined for the digital age: contemplative, precise, and endlessly curious.

## Art direction

Use an inky midnight-blue canvas, warm ivory typography and restrained antique-gold lines. Compose the hero as a large circular celestial chart with orbital rings, star points and carefully drawn constellation lines. Set a graceful editorial serif against compact scientific-style labels. Create depth with a restrained field of stars and slow, quiet motion; avoid generic space gradients, card grids and stock dashboard layouts. A vertical chapter label, asymmetric margins and a long horizontal rule should make the layout feel like an observatory publication.

## Experience

1. Open with a bold invitation to reconnect with the night sky, a strong typographic hierarchy, the celestial instrument and a direct action to explore the chart.
2. Build a functional constellation explorer. Visitors can select Orion, Cygnus, or Cassiopeia and see the diagram and short interpretation update. Provide an accessible textual control for every selection. Explicitly explain that the chart is illustrative and does not represent a live sky.
3. Follow with a short manifesto about the observatory's spirit and a distinct editorial section about the ritual of observing. Include practical, general stargazing guidance without making live astronomical claims.
4. Finish with an invitation to linger and an elegant footer. Make it clear this is a fictional concept, without fake booking or email submissions.

## Quality

Make every section feel designed as a cohesive publication. Use responsive composition, keyboard-accessible controls, appropriate contrast, readable text, reduced-motion support, and clear focus states. The interactive chart must work with no dependencies or backend and remain usable on mobile. Test the selection behavior and syntax. Make the site feel polished enough to be a memorable standalone experience.
