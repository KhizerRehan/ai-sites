# Citrus Lab — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, loud and joyful brand website for **Citrus Lab**, a fictional soda company with "good vibes, bottled". It should evoke sunshine, fizz and a refusal to take itself seriously.

## Art direction

Use deep indigo ink (#27205e), hot orange (#ff6135), neon yellow (#faff39), bubblegum pink (#ff92c5) and warm cream (#fff6d9). Set the lowercase logo and huge, tightly tracked headlines in Space Grotesk, with DM Sans for copy. Draw all imagery in CSS: a hero on an orange-to-pink radial gradient with a giant citrus slice made from a conic gradient and a tilted yellow soda can carrying the logo, plus scattered yellow asterisks. Use fully rounded pills, thick indigo outlines, big circles and tilted blob shapes. Motion comes from a scrolling marquee and a colour transition on the flavour stage.

## Experience

Start with a simple nav and the hero: eyebrow, oversized headline with one yellow word, a short line and a "Meet the flavors" pill beside the can artwork. Run an indigo ticker of slogans beneath it. Introduce the brand next to a big yellow circle holding a punctuation smiley. Then build the flavour picker on pink: three toggle buttons (Orange Pop, Pink Zing, Electric Lime) that swap the tag, flavour name, description and "tastes like" note, and recolour both the stage background and the citrus slice. Follow with an indigo philosophy section beside a yellow blob reading "POP", and a yellow footer that doubles as the "say hello" destination. State in the introduction and footer that this is a fictional brand concept. No shop, cart or forms.

Under 750px stack all grids and move the can artwork below the hero copy. Track the selected flavour with aria-pressed, announce changes through a polite live region, and switch off animation and smooth scrolling under reduced-motion.
