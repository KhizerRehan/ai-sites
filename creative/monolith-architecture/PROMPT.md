# MONOLITH — Creative brief

Build a fictional architecture studio portfolio that feels like entering a quiet, monumental building. This is an editorial study in weight, light, and negative space—not a conventional agency template.

## Direction

Use a charcoal, chalk, and raw concrete palette. Anchor the first screen with a full-height architectural photograph: dramatic, sculptural cast concrete, precise geometry, deep shadow, and a tiny human scale. Overlay an exceptionally large condensed MONOLITH wordmark, partially intersecting the image, with small, precise typographic annotations. Let one muted vermilion accent punctuate the restraint. Use a distinctive typographic contrast: severe condensed sans headings and a refined serif for human, reflective text.

## Experience

Create a responsive, accessible one-page portfolio. Begin with a statement about architecture as a dialogue between mass and light. Follow with an asymmetrical selected-work gallery of three fictional projects, each with name, place, category, date and a short story. Offer filters that actually narrow projects. Add a studio manifesto and a contact invitation that uses a real mailto link instead of a fake form. Every project is clearly a fictional concept. Provide reduced-motion behavior and useful focus states.

Keep transitions calm and tactile: reveal the images, animate fine rules and respond to hover with a gentle crop change. Let the layout breathe. Avoid decorative cards, gradients, generic feature grids, and noisy motion. Use editorial hierarchy, oversized type, full-bleed visual moments, fine rules, and compelling spatial tension. Make mobile layouts as deliberate as desktop. Produce distinct, impressive architectural imagery with consistent artistic direction.
