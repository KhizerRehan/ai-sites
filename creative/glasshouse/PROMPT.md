# Glasshouse — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, luminous gallery-style website for **Glasshouse**, a fictional independent glass studio exploring light, colour and form. It should evoke molten glass cooling in a dark room: quiet, glowing and slightly unpredictable.

## Art direction

Use deep indigo-violet (#12102d), warm cream (#fff3e9), coral (#ff806c), and salmon, periwinkle and dusty-rose fills. Set headlines in regular-weight Playfair Display, large and tightly tracked with coral italic emphasis, and copy in DM Sans with small widely spaced uppercase eyebrows. Build every object in pure CSS, not images: a large translucent blown-glass orb in the hero and vessel shapes made from irregular border radii, layered pink, violet, blue and amber gradients, white highlights and inset shadows. Motion is restrained to hover colour changes and a soft transition when the glass colour changes.

## Experience

Place a transparent header over a full-height hero with the orb, a large headline, a short line and a round "Explore the work" link. Add a coral strip of three mottos. Follow with a cream introduction stating Glasshouse is a fictional studio, then "Selected forms": three tall tiles each holding a differently shaped vessel — The Bloom, The Halo, The Drift — captioned with colourway, technique and object type. Next, a salmon "light study" with four round colour swatches (Blush, Honey, Haze, Sage) that re-tint a single vessel on a dark panel and update a one-line mood description. Close with a note from the studio repeating that it is imagined, and a simple footer. Nothing is for sale and there are no forms.

Under 800px keep only the pill nav link, move the orb above the copy and stack all grids. Track the chosen colour with aria-pressed, announce the result in a polite live region, give swatches a visible focus ring, and remove transitions and smooth scrolling under reduced-motion.
