# Midnight Market — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, loud and appetising website for **Midnight Market**, a fictional late-night street food market with the line "Come hungry. Stay late." It should evoke smoke off a grill, clattering woks and plastic stools.

## Art direction

Use near-black ink (#191916), warm paper (#f3eddb), acid lime (#d7f247), tomato red (#f04a34) and cobalt blue (#274cbf). Set enormous uppercase headlines in Archivo Black, body copy in DM Sans, and small uppercase labels and tags in Space Mono. Rely on street-food photography: a full-bleed grill scene behind the hero, darkened by gradients, and three stall photos of dumplings, food over a grill and a bowl of noodle soup. Use lime tag chips, a round rotated red badge and a slightly tilted red band of oversized type. Motion: a marquee ticker and a hover zoom on stall photos.

## Experience

Overlay a transparent header on the hero (wordmark, anchor links, the last one outlined). The hero fills the viewport with a lime eyebrow, the headline with its last line in lime, a one-line description and the badge. Follow with a lime ticker and a story placing the market in an imaginary corner of the city. Build "Meet the makers" on black: three stall cards at staggered heights, each with a photo, a lime tag (The Dumpling Counter, The Fire Pit, The Noodle Shop) and a punchy headline, followed by the red slogan band. Close with a visit section listing when, where and the vibe in ruled rows, stating that this is a fictional concept market, then a cobalt footer. The page needs no scripting.

Make it responsive: below 700px stack all grids and hide nav links except the outlined one. Give photos alt text and lazy loading, show focus outlines on links, and remove animation and transitions for reduced-motion users.
