# Peak Play — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, energetic single-page website for **Peak Play**, a fictional neighbourhood bouldering club for first-timers and regulars alike. It should evoke chalky hands, bright plastic holds and a gym where nobody takes themselves too seriously.

## Art direction

Use hot orange (#ff562e), cobalt blue (#283eea), cream (#f5f0df), near-black ink (#12181e) and acid lime (#d8f134) in big flat bands. Set headlines in Barlow Condensed at its heaviest weight, uppercase, tightly leaded and huge; set body copy in DM Sans. Draw the imagery in CSS: a blue climbing wall cut on a diagonal with panel seams, scattered blob-shaped holds with inner shading and a round rotated stamp. Use pill buttons and 2px ink rules. Motion is limited to smooth scrolling and a colour fade on the holds.

## Experience

Begin with a bordered header: a stacked wordmark, four links ending in a "Come climb" pill, and a Menu toggle on small screens. The orange hero reads "Less scroll. More scramble." beside the wall illustration, followed by an ink strip of lime mottos and an intro, "Big walls. Warm welcome.", with three word-stats. Build a blue "Pick your problem" section: a cream route board showing six holds, and a panel with three difficulty pills (Just starting, Getting playful, Bring it on) that change the route name, description, style and "good for" line and recolour the holds. Follow with a lime first-timer section of three numbered steps, then an ink closing section with a three-row list of vibe, dress code and invitation. State there and in the footer that Peak Play is a fictional concept.

Use semantic HTML, pressed-state pills with a live region for the route copy, an expanded-state attribute on the menu button, a single-column layout below 800px, and a reduced-motion rule that removes smooth scrolling and transitions.
