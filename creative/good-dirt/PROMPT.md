# GOOD DIRT — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, loud and friendly poster-style website for **GOOD DIRT**, a fictional neighborhood compost club that turns food scraps into soil. It should evoke a hand-pasted community flyer: cheerful, a little messy and gently funny.

## Art direction

Use acid lime (#d7fd35), near-black ink (#171a13), off-white paper (#f0f0e8), bubblegum pink (#ff92bc) and burnt orange (#f46b38), each owning a full-width colour block. Set oversized, tightly tracked headlines in Space Grotesk bold, body copy in DM Sans, and a few asides in italic Georgia. Give the hero wordmark one solid line and one outlined line. Use 2px ink borders, hard offset shadows, slightly rotated labels and stickers, and an inline SVG seedling growing from soil among scraps, mounted on a tilted paper card. Motion is limited to a scrolling ticker and button hover lifts.

## Experience

Open with a lime header (tilted logo, anchor links, a solid "Get involved" button) and a two-column hero: wordmark, short pitch, call to action and the illustrated card with a round pink sticker. Follow with a dark slogan ticker. Then a story section with a pink-highlighted phrase and a paragraph stating the club is fictional; a dark "Scraps to soil" section with three steps; a pink "Can I compost this?" guide built from four native disclosure accordions; and an orange closing section with a tilted lime poster and a button that opens a web search for local compost drop-offs in a new tab, noted as such beside a reminder that the concept is fictional. End with a dark footer and back-to-top link.

Make it responsive: below 800px collapse every grid to one column and hide nav links except the button. Use labelled navigation, visible focus outlines on links, buttons and summaries, and disable animation, transitions and smooth scrolling under reduced-motion preferences.
