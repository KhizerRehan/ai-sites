# Salt & Stars — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, atmospheric single-page website for **Salt & Stars**, a fictional desert camp for stargazers. It should evoke the hush after sunset, warm sand under a cold sky and having nowhere else to be.

## Art direction

Use deep night navy (#0b1738, #111f48), burnt orange (#fb6f38) and sand cream (#f8e8c8), with terracotta and dusk-pink tones in the scenery. Set headlines in Instrument Serif, very large with tight leading and italic emphasis; set body copy in DM Sans with small uppercase eyebrows. Build the hero as a scene: a dusk radial gradient, pinpoint stars, a glowing peach moon, a line-and-dot SVG constellation and layered SVG dunes with a tiny tent. Alternate dark and cream sections, use thin outlined pills, and keep motion to smooth scrolling and hover fills.

## Experience

Place the nav inside the full-height hero: a serif wordmark, three anchor links and a "Plan a night" pill. The hero reads "Find your place in the wide open." above two small captions. Follow with a cream "Out here, time gets softer" section that describes the camp as fictional and lists three word-stats, then a navy "From golden hour to after dark" section with three tall cards for the stages of an evening. Build an interactive sky guide: a dark star chart whose SVG stars and lines are redrawn for the chosen constellation, with three toggle buttons (Orion, Cassiopeia, Cygnus) that also swap the name and a short spotting note. Say the illustration is symbolic, not a live sky map. End with an orange closing band, "The sky is waiting.", and a footer calling it a fictional desert experience.

Use semantic HTML, pressed-state buttons, a chart title that updates with the selection, visible focus outlines, and layouts that stack below 800px with smaller type at 440px.
