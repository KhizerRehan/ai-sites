# Hush House — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, hushed and luxurious website for **Hush House**, a fictional perfume atelier built around "the art of almost nothing". It should evoke dim light, glass and something whispered rather than announced.

## Art direction

Use deep plum and aubergine (#281724, #321e30) for dark sections, pale grey-lilac paper (#f1eff0), soft silver (#c6c2c5) and dusty rose (#c6abbc). Set headlines in Italiana, a thin display serif, at very large sizes with tight leading, and use DM Sans for small uppercase, widely tracked eyebrows and body copy. Keep the layout calm: two-column sections, hairline rules, underlined text links instead of buttons. Use no photographs; draw the perfume bottles in pure CSS with translucent glass gradients, metallic caps and bordered labels, inside halo rings. Keep the page still, without animation.

## Experience

Begin with a dark navigation bar and a hero on a radial plum glow: an eyebrow naming it an imaginary atelier, the three-line headline with one word in a lighter tint, a short invitation, a text link, the large bottle labelled "The Unseen", and a vertical line of small text on the right edge. Follow with a note from the house stating it is fictional. Present the collection as three cards, each a miniature bottle on a differently tinted gradient with a name and three notes (The Unseen, Afterglow, Quiet Hours). Add a dark scent finder: three large row buttons, each a feeling with a small descriptor, that mark themselves pressed and write the matching fragrance and description into a live region. Continue with a ritual section beside an abstract panel of glowing rings, then a dark footer labelled a fictional concept.

Make it responsive: below 760px stack all columns, scale the bottle down and hide the vertical text. Label the bottle illustration and the finder options.
