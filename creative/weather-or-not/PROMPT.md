# Weather or Not — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, cheerful single-page website for **Weather or Not**, a fictional museum of looking up, all about clouds. It should evoke a bright museum poster and the simple pleasure of pausing to watch the sky.

## Art direction

Use navy ink (#1c2b43), sky blue (#76b6d5), cream (#fcf5da), tomato red (#ee4e32), sunshine yellow (#ffd54d) and sage green (#86aa86), each section in its own flat colour. Set big, tightly tracked headlines in bold Space Grotesk and body copy in DM Sans. Give everything a sticker-like feel: two-pixel navy outlines, hard offset shadows, pill buttons that invert on hover, a rotated circular stamp, and inline SVG illustrations with faces (a smiling cloud with raindrops, a smiling sun). No photography.

## Experience

Use a ruled header with a stacked two-colour wordmark, two anchor links and an outlined "Come on in" pill. The blue hero says "Look up. Wonder a little." beside the tilted illustration and stamp, followed by a navy ribbon of phrases. Add an intro explaining it is a make-believe museum with no ticket required. Then a yellow collection of three outlined cards (Cumulus, Cirrus, Stratus), each with its own SVG drawing on a coloured panel, a short description and a personality tag. Next, a red-orange cloud finder: a note that it is a friendly first guess, not a weather forecast, beside a card with three toggle buttons (puffy and rounded, thin and wispy, a flat layer) that reveal the matching cloud name and a sentence about it in a live region. Close with a green invitation to go outside and a navy footer describing a small imaginary museum.

Make it responsive: on small screens keep only the navigation pill and stack all grids. Use pressed states on the finder buttons, text alternatives for the illustrations and clear focus outlines.
