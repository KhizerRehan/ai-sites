# The Other Side — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, loud single-page website for **The Other Side**, a fictional skate collective where all wheels are welcome. It should evoke photocopied zines, sticker-covered decks and a crew that cheers the attempt as much as the landing.

## Art direction

Use electric blue (#143ce8), ink black (#101010), off-white paper (#f1f1e9) and acid lime (#d4ff40). Set headlines in Archivo Black, uppercase, tightly tracked and enormous, with the hero headline rotated a few degrees; set body text in DM Sans. Give it a zine feel: thick 3px black rules, a halftone dot texture over coloured sections, hard offset shadows, a rotated outlined tag and a circular stamp. Illustrate with one inline SVG: a skateboard lying across a spiky lime starburst. Buttons are lime blocks whose hard shadow shifts on hover. A lime marquee scrolls continuously.

## Experience

Start with a black utility bar that labels the site a fictional collective, then a bordered nav with a stacked wordmark and three anchor links. The blue hero reads "Find your line." with a "No gatekeeping ever" stamp and a button to the spots. After the marquee, add a manifesto section, "Not a club. A crew." Build an interactive spot guide on black: three large toggle buttons (The Landing, Blue Banks, The Underpass) beside a blue detail panel that swaps its tag, title, description, terrain and vibe for the chosen spot. Note beneath it that the places are fictional. Follow with "the code", three short community rules, then a blue closing invitation, "See you on the other side.", and a black footer repeating that this is a fictional concept.

Use semantic HTML, pressed-state toggle buttons with a live detail region, a stacked single-column layout below 800px, and a reduced-motion rule that stops the marquee and smooth scrolling.
