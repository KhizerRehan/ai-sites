# Second Life — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, quietly elegant single-page website for **Second Life**, a fictional furniture restoration atelier. It should evoke worn wood in warm light, patient handwork and affection for objects that have already lived a little.

## Art direction

Use a linen paper tone (#f0e9dd), dark walnut browns (#2b201a, #39291f), a rust accent (#a54d35) and taupe hairlines (#a49583). Set headlines in Playfair Display at regular weight with tight tracking and rust italic emphasis; set body copy in DM Sans with small uppercase eyebrows. Compose it like an interiors magazine: a split hero, generous margins, thin rules, square-cornered buttons and a staggered two-up gallery. Use warm photography of chairs and lived-in interiors loaded from Unsplash, with a caption and rotated circular stamp over the hero image. Keep motion to smooth scrolling and a button hover.

## Experience

Open with a rust announcement strip and a header with a serif wordmark, four anchor links and a Menu toggle for small screens. The hero reads "Good things deserve another chapter." beside a tall photo, followed by a rust band of three italic mottos. Add a philosophy section that calls the site a fictional studio concept, then "Objects with stories", two captioned project photos at different heights. Build an interactive repair guide on a dark split panel: three pill buttons (It feels wobbly, The finish is tired, The fabric is worn) that swap a short titled recommendation, with a note that the guide is illustrative and no substitute for inspection. Continue with a three-step numbered process, a photo-backed closing banner, "A little care goes a long way.", repeating the fictional label, and a dark footer.

Use semantic HTML, descriptive alt text, lazy-loaded gallery images, pressed-state buttons with a live result region, an expanded-state attribute on the menu toggle, and layouts that stack below 800px.
