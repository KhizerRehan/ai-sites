# STEEP — Creative Brief

> Reconstructed from the built site on 2026-11-27. The original prompt was not saved.

Create a distinctive, cinematic single-page website for **STEEP**, a fictional tea brand built around a more thoughtful tea ritual. It should evoke steam rising in low light, leaves slowly unfurling and a quiet moment kept for yourself.

## Art direction

Use a near-black green (#101c17), warm cream (#eee9db) and muted gold (#c4aa7d), plus moss green. Set headlines in Playfair Display with gold italic emphasis; use DM Sans for body copy, widely tracked uppercase eyebrows and the letterspaced wordmark. Lead with dark, moody tea photography under left-to-right gradients. Use outlined rectangular buttons that fill on hover, hairline rules and slow fades.

## Experience

Overlay a transparent header on a full-height hero ("A moment, beautifully steeped.") with two calls to action. Next, a full-bleed flavour stage: three toggle buttons (Golden Assam, Quiet Garden, Evening Bloom) swap the background photograph and update the tea type, title, description, character and sample steep time, with the copy fading up and the image easing in; an "Explore this tea" button opens its details. Follow with a split "From leaf to liquid gold." panel that reveals on scroll, then a cream collection of three photo cards, each opening a native dialog showing the tea's type, name, tasting note and sample brewing guidance, closable by button, backdrop click or Escape. Add a ritual section pairing a photograph with three numbered steps, a centred brand note on moss green, a photo-backed closing invitation, and a footer stating STEEP is a fictional tea brand. No shop or forms.

Make it responsive: on small screens replace the links with a "Menu" button that toggles a dropdown, and stack every grid. Use pressed states, a live region for the flavour copy, visible focus outlines, and switch off scene animations, scroll reveal and transitions under reduced motion.
