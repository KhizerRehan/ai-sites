# NOCTURNE — An independent cinema after dark

Create a distinctive, cinematic website for **Nocturne**, a fictional independent arthouse cinema. It should feel like discovering a lovingly made film journal, a worn ticket, and an underground cinema poster at once. Create a deeply atmospheric, high-contrast experience rather than a conventional multiplex template.

## Creative direction

- Near-black ink, warm projector-white, and a sharp electric vermilion accent.
- A commanding typographic identity: dramatically oversized condensed headlines, refined editorial serif, small monospaced metadata, fine rules, and an unmistakable sense of rhythm.
- Use a film still as the stage, supported by grain, halation, hard crop, and a tilted ticket-like screening card. Do not imitate a particular film or claim to represent real events.
- A horizontal kinetic marquee should evoke film leader. Motion must be restrained, purposeful, and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Give the page an expressive editorial layout: a sparse poster-like hero, a compact screening program, an atmospheric manifesto, an invitation to visit, and a cinematic footer. Each section should feel deliberately composed.

## Content and behavior

- Make the concept explicit: the cinema, films, times, location and prices are fictional.
- Feature a fictional film called *The Last Light* in the hero, with a clear link to the program.
- Present a curated program of fictional films with genres, runtimes, and screening times. Provide functional filter controls for All, Tonight, and This Week.
- Add a sample screening detail interaction; clearly identify the ticket experience as a demo, without processing payments or claiming real bookings.
- Include an accessible mobile navigation, keyboard-operable controls and useful focus states.
- Ensure the layout is responsive and gorgeous at desktop and mobile sizes. Keep type legible and interactions clear.
- Design and implement a complete working website, not a static mockup.
