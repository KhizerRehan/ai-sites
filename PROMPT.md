# Ember Kitchen — Creative Brief

Create a distinctive, immersive editorial website for **Ember Kitchen**, a fictional live-fire cooking journal. It should evoke the glow of embers, the sound of a crackling fire, and the ritual of cooking slowly for people you love.

## Art direction

Use charcoal black, hot ember orange, warm ivory and muted ash. Compose the page like an independent food magazine: oversized condensed typography, delicate serif captions, horizontal rules, strong contrast, asymmetrical grids and large cinematic food photography. The hero should feel like an open fire at dusk: rich, tactile, dramatic and genuinely appetizing. Use carefully paced motion and make the page feel grounded rather than a generic restaurant template.

## Experience

Lead with an editorial hero and clear invitation to explore the recipes. Follow it with a short story about cooking with fire, then an interactive recipe journal. Provide a set of fictional sample recipes with meaningful categories and accessible filter controls. Selecting a recipe should reveal ingredients and method in an accessible dialog; allow escape and close. Add a separate section on the rhythm of live-fire cooking and a closing invitation. Label the site and recipes as a fictional concept. Do not claim real bookings, events, or services.

Create a responsive, accessible site with semantic HTML, readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, clear focus states and reduced-motion support. Use food imagery that reinforces the editorial concept. Check JavaScript and the filtering and dialog behavior before deployment.
