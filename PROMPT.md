# Solstice — creative website brief

Build a complete, responsive website for **Solstice**, a fictional neighborhood bakery whose guiding idea is “a little sunshine, baked daily.” It should feel as if a joyful printed bakery wrapper came to life: tactile, handmade, generous, playful, and visually unforgettable.

## Art direction

Use a confident sun-yellow, raspberry red, inky burgundy and warm paper palette. Combine expressive, oversized display typography with a crisp readable sans-serif, irregular hand-drawn lines, sunbursts, stamps, flour-dusted textures, and mouthwatering illustrated baked goods. Make the composition asymmetric and editorial, with visual surprises and a distinctive hero—not a standard centered marketing template. Keep the design warm and premium rather than childish.

## Experience

- Open with an oversized personality-filled headline, hand-rendered pastry artwork, a clear menu action, and a concise explanation of the fictional bakery.
- Offer a dynamic menu where visitors can filter between bread, pastry, and all items. Include realistic sample descriptions and prices; mark the menu as an illustrative concept, not live stock or ordering.
- Tell a short story of the bakery’s daily ritual with a contrasting visual rhythm, and add a visit section with fictional sample hours and location clearly marked as a concept.
- Include navigation, clear links, responsive layouts, tasteful motion and hover states, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
- Avoid misleading functional checkout, orders, reservations, or newsletter submissions.

## Craft and delivery

Use custom food imagery that looks like hand-painted gouache on textured paper, not emoji or generic icons. Build a complete experience with polished typography, thoughtful mobile layouts, accessible contrast, and functional menu filtering. Test the interaction and static output. Publish privately as a fictional demo.
