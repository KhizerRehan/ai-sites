# TIDEPOOL — Creative brief

Create an immersive interactive field guide to a fictional deep-sea expedition, designed as an editorial journey rather than a conventional marketing page. The site should feel like a luminous scientific archive glimpsed through dark water: elegant, mysterious, tactile and quietly alive.

Use an ink-black and abyssal blue palette punctuated by acid-lime and icy cyan bioluminescence. Pair monumental high-contrast serif typography with a precise, understated sans-serif for labels. Layer atmospheric underwater imagery, fine specimen annotations, delicate lines and subtle drifting particles. Give the opening view the scale of a museum installation, with an oversized living specimen, dramatic negative space and a clear invitation to descend.

Create a complete and responsive experience: an opening expedition statement, a depth explorer that lets visitors select a zone and updates its specimen and descriptive field notes, a curated specimen archive with accessible filters, a short editorial story about life after sunlight, and a considered closing invitation. Build meaningful interactions that work with mouse, touch and keyboard. Provide reduced-motion support, text contrast, accessible controls and a polished mobile layout.

Treat all expedition identity, collection entries and copy as fictional illustrative content. Do not suggest that the site operates a real scientific institution. The result should feel distinct from a generic card-grid website, with irregular editorial composition, strong photography, ambient movement and restrained technical detail.
