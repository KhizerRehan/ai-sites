# The Ultimate Burger — cinematic animation

Create a dramatic, seamless looping hero animation for The Burger Club. On arrival, a toasted bottom bun anchors the scene. Lettuce, tomato, a chargrilled beef patty, melted cheddar, red onion, and the sesame top bun arrive in that order, each settling into a complete burger. Give the ingredients a sense of weight and keep their textures and the warm studio lighting prominent.

Hold the fully assembled burger for roughly two seconds. Then burst the ingredients outward in different directions, with crumbs and sauce droplets flying through the air. Make the explosion energetic and cinematic, with varied trajectories and rotation. Clear the scene and repeat the build. Keep the wordmark and headline readable throughout, and let visitors pause the animation. For reduced motion, show the assembled burger as a static composition.
